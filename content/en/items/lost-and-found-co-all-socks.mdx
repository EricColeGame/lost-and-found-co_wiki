## The Sock Hunt Is Really a Morning Madness Request

The last sock in **Lost and Found Co.** can turn a cute hidden-object case into a full-house stare-down. Players describe the sock search as part of the **Morning Madness** request, where the goal is to get everyone ready, and the missing socks are tucked into plants, plushies, and a hidden agent room rather than gathered in one laundry basket. The sources reviewed here do not include an official, complete sock map, but a [Steam Community discussion about socks in Morning Madness](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390/discussions/0/806845127026945402/) documents several stubborn locations and the route other players used to find them.

Lost and Found Co. is the hidden-object adventure starring Ducky, a duck-turned-human intern at Goddess Mei's company, according to the [official Lost and Found Co. site](https://lostandfoundcogame.com/). That context matters: the game wants you to inspect props, not just sweep the floor. If you are trying to collect all socks in one pass, slow down and treat the room like a series of small search zones.

## How to Find the Secret Agent Room

The secret agent room is the most repeated answer for a stubborn sock. In the Steam thread, one player asked where the room was, and the reply was "Dog house." That is the only direction given in the discussion, so start your search at the dog house instead of re-clearing the main bedrooms.

If you have already checked the dog house and still cannot get inside, step back and look for an interaction point rather than a normal doorway. Hidden-object games often hide entrances inside props, and Lost and Found Co. uses that trick often. The general [Casual Game Guides walkthrough for Lost and Found Co.](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) notes that objects can be tucked into signs, shelf edges, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes that need an extra click. The same prop-first mindset applies here: check the dog house itself, not just the area around it.

## A Practical Search Order for the Last Sock

If you are down to one sock, avoid running through the whole house again. Use the player-reported spots as a short list, then widen your sweep.

1. Check the first-floor outdoor edge. Look in the tomato plants near the open gate.
2. Move to the second-floor bedrooms. Check the green plant in the room with the green bed, then the duck plush on the pink bed.
3. Visit the dog house and look for the secret agent room. This is the spot most likely to be missed if you have been searching only the main house.
4. Rescan every plant and soft prop. Plants and plushies account for several last-sock reports, so they deserve a second pass.
5. Zoom in and pan across cluttered areas. The general Lost and Found Co. walkthrough recommends scanning in layers and opening props instead of clicking blindly.
6. Use hints late. Juju hints are better spent after you have narrowed the problem to one room or one object type, not at the first sign of frustration.

Different players missed different socks, so another person's "last sock" spot may not be your last sock. That is why the list works best as a checklist, not a guaranteed order.

## What to Do If You Still See Nine Socks

One player in the thread reported finding only nine socks, while another said their ninth was in the secret agent room. The discussion does not settle an exact total, and it does not prove that socks spawn in a fixed order. What it does show is that the secret agent room and plants are common blind spots.

If your counter is stuck, do a targeted reset: leave the main bedrooms, check the dog house entrance, then sweep every plant on both floors. Do not assume a room is finished just because you found the obvious sock on the bed. In this game, the final object is often the one sitting in a prop you have already looked at but not opened or zoomed in on.
