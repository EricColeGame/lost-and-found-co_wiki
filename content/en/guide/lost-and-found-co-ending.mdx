## What Actually Happens in the Lost and Found Co Ending?

If you are hunting for the Lost and Found Co ending because your to-do list still has one stubborn name on it, the short answer is this: the main story closes over two chapters. Chapter 10, "Unexpected Outcome," is the climax where you rally the town's faith so Goddess Mei can defend the shrine from Mr. Zorro. Chapter 11, "The Goddess's Return," is the epilogue — a party scene where you round up the cast one last time.

Neither chapter is a giant hidden-object hunt. Chapter 10 is mostly a chain of character finds across one street stage, and Chapter 11 is a short victory lap through the office. The details below follow the chapter 9–11 walkthrough published by [The Phrasemaker](https://thephrasemaker.com/2026/03/18/lost-and-found-co-main-story-walkthrough-chapters-9-11/), so treat the specific locations as third-party walkthrough guidance rather than official documentation.

## Chapter 10 – Unexpected Outcome: Rallying the Town's Faith

This is the level that decides whether the OK Skyview gets built on the shrine. Your job is to collect faith from everyone Ducky and Mei have met, and the game hands you the cast in a loose order. Start with the three characters closest to the shrine entrance, then sweep the street.

**The first three faith sources:**

- **Ellie** — left side of the shrine near the entrance, standing behind Kate.
- **Kenta** — just left of Ellie, next to a masked kid.
- **Klay** — near the reporter at the shrine entrance.

Once those three are in, the stage opens up and you start pulling in the wider Plumville crowd.

### The rest of the townspeople

Work the stage from the shrine outward so you do not re-check the same street twice:

- **Buff Baby** — right of Klay, behind a building with a dirty blue roof.
- **Farland Princess** — right of Kenta on the shrine's left side.
- **News Reporter** — sitting right at the shrine entrance, hard to miss.
- **Thomas Lee** — the police dog, inside the center barricades holding people back.
- **Nekoten Staff** — beside Thomas Lee, tucked near a tree's leaves.
- **Stampford** — the postman, behind a green van on the stage's right side.
- **Master Tang** — on the green roof of the fast-food restaurant in that same right-side block.
- **McCowney** — just above Stampford.
- **Chris** — the street musician, next to McCowney.
- **The Great Tanuki** — click the Tanuki figure sitting on the green taxi.
- **Mr. Orge** — at the corner of the flower stand on the right side of the street.
- **Flower Shop Staff** — below the flower shop with her colleagues, near the mascots.
- **Mr. Hotwings** — on the left-most street of the stage.
- **Afrodite** — at the mouth of the alley between two buildings, where a shark is eating.
- **Magical Girl** — on the roof by the shrine's corner.

A few of these are obscured finds rather than plain clicks. The Great Tanuki in particular only appears after you interact with the taxi prop, which is the pattern the game uses throughout: if a character name makes no sense in open space, look for the clickable prop they are hiding in.

### How the climax resolves

With everyone's faith gathered, Mei defends the shrine. Mr. Zorro backs down and cancels the OK Skyview development, which closes out the main conflict. According to the walkthrough, this is the point where the main story is officially finished and only the epilogue remains.

## Chapter 11 – The Goddess's Return: The Epilogue

Chapter 11 opens after Mr. Zorro's surrender, with Ducky and friends throwing a party for Mei's return from a nap that lasted several days. There is no puzzle pressure here — it is a character-collection sequence set in and around the Lost and Found Co. office.

**Where everyone is waiting:**

- **Kenta, Kate, Ellie, Klay, and Kit** — all in the lobby room. Kit also shares his history with Mei if you talk to him.
- **McChonk and Chisel** — in the room on the left.
- **Mallet** — on the balcony of the office building.
- **Mei** — the one that trips people up. Enter Mei's Parlor first; she is not there. Then go back inside the office and head upstairs to the balcony near your room's entrance.

Finding Mei is the trigger. Once everyone is assembled and the party starts, the story ends on a group illustration of the happy cast. That is the actual final beat of Lost and Found Co — no post-credits boss, no hidden final level required to see it.

## Tips for Cleaning Up the Last Few Objectives

If you are stuck one name short in Chapter 10, the usual hidden-object habits apply. The [Casual Game Guides walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) breaks the objective types into three categories worth remembering:

- **Obscured** objectives hide inside or behind a clickable prop. Open the box, drawer, sign, or vehicle before assuming the target is missing.
- **Quest** objectives unlock more targets after you find them, so a room is not finished just because one chain item vanished.
- **Juju** are optional collectibles disguised as ordinary objects, and catching them earns extra hints for later searches.

Two practical habits help in the crowded street stage. First, scan in layers — rooftops and hanging props, then storefronts and counters, then ground clutter — instead of clicking randomly. Second, save your Juju hints until you have narrowed the problem to one zone, because a hint is wasted on a room you have not swept yet. The same guide notes that awkward hitboxes and partially visible objects cause most misses, so click the center of small items rather than their edges.

## Is That Really the End? Updates and the Switch Release

The main story ends at Chapter 11, but Lost and Found Co has kept growing since launch. Official [Lost and Found Co. patch notes on Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390) list a 1.2.1 update dated October 14 that added items to the Kit Shop, adjusted localization, and fixed stage issues in the Lobby, Love After Life, and Fangtastic Feast — so players who finish the campaign may still have side stages and optional objectives waiting.

The same Steam community hub carries an October 28 announcement confirming the game is coming to Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch in early 2027, with up to four-player co-op, Joy-Con 2 mouse controls, motion controls, and touchscreen support. Nothing in that announcement changes the ending itself; it just means more players will reach it.

## FAQ

**How many chapters does Lost and Found Co have?**
The main story runs through Chapter 11, with Chapter 10 serving as the climax and Chapter 11 as the epilogue. Later content updates added separate stages that sit outside that main arc.

**Do I need to find every optional objective to see the Lost and Found Co ending?**
No. The finale triggers on the main faith-gathering chain in Chapter 10 and the character finds in Chapter 11. Optional objectives and Juju are separate collection goals.

**Why can't I find Mei in Chapter 11?**
She is not in Mei's Parlor. Enter the parlor first to trigger the check, then return to the office and go upstairs to the balcony near your room's entrance.

**What happens to Mr. Zorro at the end?**
After the town's faith powers Mei, Mr. Zorro concedes and drops the plan to build the OK Skyview on the shrine. The epilogue then shows the cast celebrating Mei's return.
