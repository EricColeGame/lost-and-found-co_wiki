Lost and Found Co. is a cozy hidden-object adventure, so your finish time depends far more on how thoroughly you click than on reflexes or difficulty spikes. The short answer: HowLongToBeat currently lists roughly **8½ hours for the main story, 13 hours for the main story plus side objectives, and about 18 hours for a completionist run**. Those figures rest on a small pool of player submissions, so treat them as a range rather than a promise.

The spread is wide. On PC, logged runs range from a fastest finish of **6 hours** to a slowest of **32 hours**, which tells you more about this game than any single average. The site also shows an 82% rating and 58 players marked as having beaten it.

## Why the Sample Size Matters Here

The completionist figure is the most reliable number on the page, because it draws on 30 submissions with an average near 18½ hours and a median around 17¾. The main-story figure, by contrast, comes from a single logged run of 6 hours, which is why the summary and the polled average do not perfectly agree.

In plain terms:

- Expect **8 to 12 hours** if you chase the main objectives and little else.
- Expect **12 to 16 hours** if you clear side objectives as you go.
- Expect **17 to 25 hours** if you want every objective, every reward, and a decorated room.

Those ranges are estimates built from the reported data, not official figures from the developer.

## What Makes Playtimes Swing So Widely

Three design choices stretch or shrink a run more than anything else.

**There is no time limit.** The [Boiling Steam review of Lost and Found Co.](https://boilingsteam.com/lost-and-found-co/) notes that the game never pressures you with a clock, which is exactly why one player's relaxed session can run twice as long as another's.

**Optional objectives are unhinted.** Main objectives sit in the HUD and can be clicked for help, but side objectives appear only on a checklist and offer no guidance. A reviewer for RPGamer also found the hint system unreliable at times, particularly for those tougher optional targets.

**Everything is clickable.** Scenes are packed with animated characters and props that respond to clicks, and some interactions trigger hidden bonus events. Curiosity is rewarded, and it is also the single biggest time sink in the game.

## How the Campaign Is Structured

According to [Wikipedia's overview of Lost and Found Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_and_Found_Co.), the main story spans **eleven chapters**, each centered on a client who needs help from the business, across **more than fifteen stages** that you can revisit at any time. Short animated interludes between chapters carry the plot forward.

Post-launch support has added to that count. A June 2026 summer update introduced the stages "Master of Findjutsu" and "My Summer Memories 2," and an October 1, 2026 update added "Love After Life" and "Fangtastic Feast" alongside Korean localization and a dialogue history feature. Anyone comparing their time to an older review should keep those additions in mind — a reviewer who reached 100% of objectives across 16 scenarios in about 10 hours did so back in March 2026, before the extra stages arrived.

## How to Beat Lost and Found Co. Faster

If you want to keep your playtime near the main-story estimate, these habits help:

- **Clear HUD objectives first.** They are the only required content, and clicking one gives you a hint at no cost.
- **Spend hint points carefully.** A second, more direct hint consumes a point, and you refill points by finding the greyed-out creatures disguised around each map.
- **Save side objectives for a revisit.** Stages can be replayed, so there is no reason to stall a story run hunting a single missing item.
- **Postpone decorating.** Ducky's room and the office park are pure customization; furniture from Kit can wait until the credits roll.
- **Use the mouse-only option.** The game is playable with a mouse alone, which many players find quicker for dense scenes than scanning with a controller.

## FAQ

**How long to beat Lost and Found Co. if I skip side content?**
HowLongToBeat's main-story estimate is 8½ hours, though it is based on a single submission, and the fastest logged PC run is 6 hours. Skipping optional objectives and room decorating is the quickest path through the campaign.

**Does Lost and Found Co. have a time limit or fail states?**
No. The game imposes no clock, and searching slowly carries no penalty. Hints cost points rather than time, and you refill those points by finding disguised creatures in each scene.

**How long does 100% completion take?**
Plan on roughly 18 hours based on 30 completionist submissions, with a median near 17¾ hours. If you linger over decorations and hidden interactions, 25 hours or more is realistic.

**Is Lost and Found Co. longer than other hidden-object games?**
It is a full campaign rather than a level pack: eleven chapters, more than fifteen stages, and side objectives layered on top. That structure puts it well past a typical single-sitting hidden-object title, while staying short enough to finish in a handful of evenings.
