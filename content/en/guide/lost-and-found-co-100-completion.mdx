## Why Lost and Found Co. 100% Completion Is Simpler Than It Looks

You are one missing object away from a perfect stage, and the game refuses to tell you which one. That is the usual hidden-object wall — but in Lost and Found Co., the road to full completion is unusually clean: every achievement is tied to fully clearing a specific stage, with no speedrun tricks, no hidden combat challenge, and no missable route split to worry about.

The catch is that "fully clearing" means everything in a stage, not just the main case. You need the story objective, the optional goals, and the Juju, all finished, before the stage stops showing greyed-out entries.

One important limit up front: Steam currently lists **31 achievements** for the game. An earlier community-maintained completion list, published by [Casual Game Guides' Lost and Found Co. achievement page](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/completion-achievements) on April 19, 2026, counted **27 achievements** in the Steam build at that time. The game has received content updates since (including two added levels in a June 2026 update), so treat 27 as a dated snapshot and confirm the live count on your own achievement screen.

## How Stage Completion and Achievements Actually Connect

The structure is straightforward once you see it. Each stage hands you a main objective, optional objectives layered inside the same scene, and Juju — hidden helpers disguised as ordinary props that also grant extra hints. The achievement pops when the stage reads 100%, which means nothing left unfinished.

According to the [Steam Community guide hub for Lost and Found Co.](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390/guides/), the most-used player references are exactly what you would expect for this structure: full main-and-optional objective lists, complete Juju location maps, and video walkthroughs per level. That tells you where completion chasers actually get stuck — the optional layer, not the story.

A few naming quirks are worth knowing before you start hunting a phantom achievement:

- Some achievement labels use shorter or older names than the stage pages do. If a name looks unfamiliar, trust the stage it is attached to.
- Request-style stages are side jobs, and only some of them carry their own achievement.
- Juju are optional in tone but count toward a clean stage, so leaving them behind keeps a stage permanently unfinished.

## The Fastest Route to 31 Steam Achievements

The verified advice from the completion guide is refreshingly undramatic: do not chase perfection on a first visit. Learn the rooms first, then clean up.

1. **Finish the story once.** Play through without stressing about optional objectives. You will learn how this game hides things — inside sign edges, shelf lips, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes that need an extra click before the real target appears.
2. **Replay stages with unfinished side tasks while the room is still fresh.** Memory of layout is the single biggest time-saver in a hidden-object game.
3. **Hold your Juju until the end.** They are hint currency, and they are wasted on a room you have only half-searched. Spend them when you are down to the last stubborn object or the last unsearched zone.
4. **Save the clutter-heavy maps for calm sessions.** The guide flags Hop Street, Jolly Jug Isle, Nekoten Cafe, Breezy Hill Heights, Leo Family's Home, and Kenta's House as the busiest cleanup runs.
5. **Verify the achievement count against the live build.** Steam currently lists 31 achievements, while the April 2026 guide documented 27 — check the [official Lost and Found Co. Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2101390/) and its global achievement stats for the current total.

## Cleanup Tactics for the Last Stubborn Object

When you are stuck on one item, clicking faster never helps. Change how you search instead.

- **Scan in layers.** Skyline and hanging props first, then shelves and counters, then floor clutter. On multi-floor maps, clear one zone completely before moving on — half-searching five areas is how objects vanish from memory.
- **Open things.** Obscured objectives are hidden inside or behind a clickable prop. If the clue feels vague, the answer is usually to open the container first.
- **Do not trust a finished-looking room on a quest chain.** Quest objectives unlock additional targets after you find the first one, so one disappearing item does not mean the stage is done.
- **Trust shape over wording.** Translation differences can make a clue sound strange, but the art plays fair. A folded bill can register as a wallet; a tiny prop can sit in a shelf seam.

For room-by-room routes, the [Casual Game Guides Lost and Found Co. walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) organizes the game into story cases, requests, and optional objectives, which maps neatly onto the order described above.

## Community-Reported Bugs Worth Knowing Before a Cleanup Run

One known issue circulates among completion chasers. A Steam guide by 小蓝洗衣机, updated June 24, 2026 alongside two added levels, warns that a shuriken mission can fail to register if you trigger a shuriken on the tower **before** accepting the mission from the NPC. The guide's advice is to talk to the NPC as soon as you enter the map, and it notes the bug had been reported and was awaiting a fix at the time of writing. That is a community report, not an official confirmation — check whether your build still behaves this way before relying on it.

Official patch notes show that stage-level fixes do ship regularly. The October 14, 2026 patch (version 1.2.1) included a stage soft-lock fix for Love After Life and a fix for a slime objective that failed to display correctly when re-entering Fangtastic Feast. Keeping the game updated is genuinely part of a clean completion run.

## FAQ

**How many achievements does Lost and Found Co. have?**
Steam currently lists 31 achievements. An earlier community completion list counted 27 in the Steam build as of April 19, 2026, with each one tied to 100% completion of a specific stage. Because the game has added stages since then, confirm the current total on your own achievement screen.

**Is Lost and Found Co. 100% completion missable?**
No. The completion guide states there are no missable route splits and no secret challenges — you can replay stages to clean up anything you skipped. The only thing you can "miss" is the achievement for a stage you never finish.

**Do I need Juju for 100% completion?**
Juju count toward a fully cleared stage, so a room with Juju still hidden is not finished. They double as hint currency, which is why saving them for the last stubborn object is better than spending them on a room you have not properly searched yet.

**Which stages do not have their own achievement?**
In the April 2026 snapshot, Clubhouse of the Dead, The End of Summer Memories, The Stumbling Steps, Fangtastic Feast, Master of Findjutsu, Finding Familiars, and Duckinator 3029 had no separate Steam achievement. Clear them if you enjoy a clean room, but do not hunt an achievement that is not there.
