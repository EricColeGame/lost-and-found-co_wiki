## How Lost and Found Co Achievements Actually Work

If you are stuck at 30 out of 31, the problem is probably not your eyes — it is your cleanup order. In *Lost and Found Co.*, the Steam achievements are not tied to speed, combat, or hidden route splits. According to the [100% completion and achievement list from Casual Game Guides](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/completion-achievements), every achievement in the build they documented is awarded for reaching **100% completion on a specific stage**. That means your checklist for any achievement is the same every time: finish the main objective, clear the optional objectives, catch the Juju, and leave nothing greyed out.

The catch is that the list is dated. That guide was published April 19, 2026, and the game has shipped stage content since — including a June 24, 2026 update that added two levels and an Autumn update in October. Treat any published count as a snapshot and confirm your remaining unlocks in the Steam global achievements panel before you start replaying stages.

## Fastest Route to Full Stage Completion

The least painful path is the least dramatic one. Play the story first so you learn how each room hides things, then come back for cleanup runs while the layout is still fresh in your head.

- **Clear the main objective first.** Story Cases push progression; Requests behave like side jobs. Finish the case, then chase the extras.
- **Replay before you forget.** A stage you cleared yesterday is a stage you can finish today in half the time.
- **Save the clutter monsters for calm sessions.** The guide flags Hop Street, Jolly Jug Isle, Nekoten Cafe, Breezy Hill Heights, Leo Family's Home, and Kenta's House as the busiest cleanup runs.
- **Trust the stage, not the label.** A few achievement names are shorter or older than the stage page they belong to, so match by location rather than exact wording.

## Search Techniques That Save Your Last Object

Most "impossible" last items are hitbox problems, not perception problems. Clicking faster rarely helps; scanning in sections does.

**Work in layers.** Sweep the skyline and hanging props, then shelves and counters, then floor clutter. If a map has multiple floors or interior rooms, fully clear one zone before moving on — half-searching five areas is how items get forgotten.

**Use the movement tools more than you think.** Zoom in on tiny props, pan across packed shelves instead of clicking blind, and change floors once furniture starts stacking. The game also hides targets inside clickable props: sign edges, shelf lips, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes often need one extra click before the real item appears.

**Know your objective types.** *Obscured* objectives sit inside or behind something clickable, so open the prop before giving up. *Quest* objectives can unlock additional targets after you find the first one, so a stage is not finished the moment one chain item vanishes.

## Juju, Hints, and When to Spend Them

Juju are optional objectives disguised as ordinary objects, and catching them earns extra hints for later item hunts. That makes them both a collectible and a resource — and the fastest way to waste them is to panic-spend.

Finish the broad sweep first. Use a hint when you are down to one stubborn item or one stubborn zone and need the game to point at a corner of the room, not when a room is simply crowded. If you keep missing the same *type* of object, trust the shape over the wording: translation quirks can make an item name sound stranger than the art actually is.

## Community Guides and One Bug Worth Knowing

The [Steam Community guide hub for Lost and Found Co.](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390/guides/) is where the practical completion work lives. Highlights include a full main-and-optional objective list by tullybunny, video walkthroughs for every map by Geralt and AlexPrime, screenshot-based item locations by AtlasofStars, a requests-focused guide by Deus Mama, and dedicated Juju location guides by Lotus and Bliccer.

One reported issue is worth flagging. In a Steam guide covering the June 24, 2026 update, author 小蓝洗衣机 warns that a shuriken mission can fail to count if you trigger a shuriken on the tower *before* accepting the mission from the NPC. Their advice is to talk to the NPC as soon as you enter the map. The bug was reported at the time of that guide, so check whether a later patch fixed it before planning around it.

For official patch notes and store details, the [Lost and Found Co. Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2101390/) is the primary source — including the October 14 patch (1.2.1) that fixed a To-do UI lockout after quest unlocks and a stage soft lock in Love After Life.

## FAQ

**How many achievements does Lost and Found Co. have?**
Steam currently lists 31. Casual Game Guides counted 27 in the Steam build as of April 19, 2026, with each one tied to 100% completion of a specific stage. Because later updates added stages, verify the current total in your Steam global achievements list rather than trusting a fixed number.

**Do I need to find every Juju for 100% completion?**
The completion route described in this Lost and Found Co achievement guide includes catching the Juju alongside main and optional objectives. Since achievements trigger on full stage completion, treat Juju as part of the clear rather than a bonus.

**What is the fastest way to earn all the achievements?**
Finish the story once, then replay stages with unfinished side tasks while the room layout is fresh. Use Juju hints only when you are down to the last object, and save the densest maps — Hop Street, Jolly Jug Isle, Nekoten Cafe, Breezy Hill Heights, Leo Family's Home, Kenta's House — for relaxed cleanup sessions.

**Is there a bug that can block completion?**
A Steam guide author reported that triggering a shuriken on the tower before accepting the related mission could prevent it from counting, potentially blocking the level. Accept the NPC mission first, and check the patch notes for a fix.
