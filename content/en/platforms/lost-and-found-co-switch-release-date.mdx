## The Short Answer: Early 2027, No Exact Day Yet

If you are waiting on the **Lost and Found Co Switch release date**, here is everything that has actually been confirmed: the cozy hidden object adventure from Thai studio Bit Egg Inc. is headed to both **Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 in early 2027**. That is the whole announcement — a window, not a date.

There is no confirmed day, no confirmed price, and no open pre-orders for either Nintendo platform as of November 2, 2026. Anyone quoting a specific date right now is guessing. The good news is that the game itself is finished, polished, and already playable on PC, so the wait is about porting and certification rather than development.

## What "Early 2027" Actually Means

"Early 2027" is a publisher's shorthand, not a commitment. In practice, a window like this usually points to the first quarter of the calendar year, but Bit Egg and publisher Gamirror Games have not narrowed it further. Three things remain unannounced:

- **The exact release day.** No date has been given for either Switch model.
- **Pricing.** The PC version is listed at $17.99 standard, but Nintendo eShop pricing has not been confirmed.
- **Whether both versions launch together.** The announcement pairs Switch and Switch 2, but simultaneous launch has not been spelled out.

It is also unclear whether the Switch release will ship with the post-launch content the PC version has received. Treat that as an open question until the studio says otherwise.

## What Switch Players Will Be Getting

The port is not a stripped-down version of a small game. *Lost and Found Co.* is a full hidden object adventure set in the fictional town of Plumville, where Ducky — a duck transformed into a human boy by the goddess Mei — helps run a lost-property business out of a shrine.

The structure, as documented for the PC release, includes:

- **Eleven story chapters**, each built around a client searching for missing belongings.
- **More than fifteen stages**, all revisitable for missed optional objectives.
- **Optional objectives** discovered by interacting with characters and scenery, not just the main list.
- **Room decoration** for Ducky's room, with furniture bought from Kit and freely moved, resized, and layered.
- **Interactive scenes** where clicking almost anything produces a reaction.

The PC version also carries 31 Steam achievements, which hints at how much optional content is packed into those illustrated stages — though achievement parity on Switch has not been confirmed.

## Why the Port Matters More Than the Date

*Lost and Found Co.* is not a niche curiosity. It holds a 90 on Metacritic and a 90 average on OpenCritic, won the Grand Jury Award at the 2024 SEA Game Awards, took Thai Game of the Year at the 2026 Thai Game Awards, and had sold more than 100,000 copies worldwide by August 2026. Critics consistently praised the hand-drawn animation and the density of detail in each scene.

That makes the Switch versions a natural fit. A single-player, mouse-optional, no-quick-time-events hidden object game is exactly the kind of title that suits handheld play, and the PC build is already Steam Deck Verified — a reasonable, if unofficial, signal that the design translates to a small screen. The Switch 2 version, in particular, is the one to watch for anyone who wants the sharpest version of those crowded, animated scenes.

The PC version has also kept growing. A June 2026 summer update added the stages "Master of Findjutsu" and "My Summer Memories 2" plus Japanese and German localisation, and an October 1, 2026 update added "Love After Life," "Fangtastic Feast," Korean localisation, dialogue history, and new decoration items. The Steam release now supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Thai, Japanese, German, and Korean.
