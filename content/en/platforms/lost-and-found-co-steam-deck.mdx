## How Lost and Found Co Performs on the Steam Deck

The most detailed hands-on Deck testing comes from [BoilingSteam's review](https://boilingsteam.com/lost-and-found-co/), published March 16, 2026. The reviewer described Deck play as "satisfactory" overall but flagged that the game is more demanding than its storybook art suggests.

Key observations from that review:

- **Battery draw:** busy scenarios pulled roughly **17Wh**, which the reviewer characterized as power-hungry for this type of game.
- **Frame rate:** zooming out on a densely populated scene dropped performance from about **90 fps to 45 fps**.
- **Workaround:** capping the screen at **30Hz** helped extend battery life.
- **Verdict:** playable and enjoyable on Deck, though the reviewer personally preferred a big screen with family.

Those numbers come from one reviewer's testing on their own unit, so your results will vary with the scene, your zoom level, and which Deck model you own.

### Why the busiest scenes matter most

Lost and Found Co packs its levels with animated, clickable objects — characters moving, props reacting, ambient animations running. The [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2101390/Lost_and_Found_Co/) emphasizes "thousands of interactive objects," and that density is exactly what stresses a handheld.

The good news: there's **no time limit**, per BoilingSteam's playthrough, so a frame dip in a crowded scene never costs you a fail state. It's a pacing annoyance, not a gameplay threat.

### A tip for spotting tiny items

Because hidden-object hunting depends on spotting small shapes, use the Deck's zoom and don't be afraid to sit closer to the screen. BoilingSteam noted the zoomed-in view looked and ran noticeably better than the zoomed-out overview, so lean on the closer camera when you're stuck on a stubborn objective.

## Battery and Performance Tips for Deck Players

You don't need to fiddle much, but a few adjustments help:

1. **Cap the refresh rate.** BoilingSteam found setting the screen to 30Hz helped offset the game's power draw.
2. **Stay zoomed in when you can.** The wide view is where frame rates fell hardest in testing.
3. **Play in shorter sessions.** With no timers or penalties, Lost and Found Co is easy to pick up in 20-minute chunks — which also stretches your charge across a day.
4. **Try the demo first.** A free demo is available on Steam, so you can test the feel on your own Deck before spending anything.
