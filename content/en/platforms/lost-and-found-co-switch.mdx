## Is Lost and Found Co. Coming to Nintendo Switch?

Yes — but you will have to wait a while. According to the game's release information, **Lost and Found Co.** is scheduled to arrive on both the Nintendo Switch and the Nintendo Switch 2 in **early 2027**. No specific day has been announced, and no price or pre-order details for either console version have been shared publicly.

That matters if you have been watching the game since its PC debut. The cozy hidden-object adventure from Thai studio Bit Egg Inc. launched on Windows and macOS in March 2026 to strong reviews, so Switch owners are getting a well-tested version rather than a launch-day gamble — assuming the port keeps the content intact.

## What Hasn't Been Announced for the Switch Version

- **Switch pricing.** The PC version is listed on Steam at $17.99, with a temporary discount running at the time of writing. Console pricing has not been published, so do not assume the two match.
- **Pre-orders or wishlisting.** No Nintendo eShop listing has been confirmed.
- **Performance details.** Resolution, frame rate, file size, and load times for either Switch model are unknown.
- **Control specifics.** The PC release supports a mouse-only option. Whether the Switch version uses touchscreen input, pointer controls, or standard button navigation has not been detailed.
- **Cross-save or shared progression** between PC and console. Nothing has been said either way.

If you see a specific Switch date, price, or "pre-order now" link circulating, check it against the developer's own channels before believing it.

## What Switch Players Will Be Getting

The core of the game is unchanged by platform. You play as Ducky, a duck transformed into a human boy by the goddess Mei, working at her shrine-turned-office lost-property business in the town of Plumville. Each stage is a dense, hand-drawn isometric scene packed with animated characters and clickable props.

Structurally, the campaign runs across eleven chapters and more than fifteen stages, with optional objectives layered on top of the main list. Some targets hide inside or behind interactive props, and stages can be revisited to clean up anything you missed. A decorating system lets you furnish Ducky's room with items bought from Kit the fox.

Two content updates have already shipped on PC, and both would presumably be included in a 2027 console release:

- **June 2026:** two new stages, "Master of Findjutsu" and "My Summer Memories 2," plus Japanese and German localization and new decor.
- **October 2026:** the Halloween-flavored stages "Love After Life" and "Fangtastic Feast," Korean localization, dialogue history, and more room items.

One practical tip carries over regardless of platform. As [Casual Game Guides' Lost and Found Co. walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) points out, scanning a scene in layers — skyline and hanging props first, then shelves, then floor clutter — beats clicking faster, and Juju collectibles act as optional helpers that feed the hint system later.
