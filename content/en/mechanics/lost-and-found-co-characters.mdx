## Lost and Found Co Characters at a Glance

The cast of *Lost and Found Co.* is small at the core and enormous at the edges. Four figures drive the story: **Ducky**, the duck-turned-human intern you play as; **Mei**, the fading war goddess who founded the business; **Kit**, the fox who supplies both a crucial potion and your room decor; and **Zorro**, the masked executive trying to tear the whole operation down.

Beyond them, the game fills Plumville with client characters who arrive chapter by chapter. Official material confirms the roles below but does not publish a complete named roster, so treat this as the verified core rather than an exhaustive list.

## Ducky: The Duck-Turned-Human Intern

Ducky is the character you actually control. According to the official premise, Ducky was a duck before Mei transformed him into a human boy, and he now works as an intern at her magical startup — a company dedicated to recovering lost items for the townspeople of Plumville.

That transformation matters mechanically as well as narratively. Ducky is the reason the game has a home base: as the business grows, Mei's shrine becomes an office and Ducky gains a room the player can decorate with furniture bought from Kit and then moved, resized, and layered.

Ducky also shows up inside stage content, not just in cutscenes. Patch notes for version 1.2.1 mention optional dialogue added for Ducky and Mei during Act 2 of the "Love After Life" stage, which tells you the leads appear in optional conversations while you are hunting for objects.

## Mei: The War Goddess Behind the Company

Mei is the engine of the plot. She is described as an ancient war goddess who has lost much of her power as the number of people worshipping her declined — and transforming Ducky into a human weakened her further.

Her plan is straightforward: run a lost-property business at her shrine, help the residents of Plumville, and attract new worshippers to restore her strength. That is also why the store description frames the goal as helping "a tiny dragon regain her power." Per the plot summary, Mei drinks a potion obtained with Kit's help and is transformed into a small dragon, so the tiny dragon and the goddess are the same character at different points in the story.

Practically, Mei is your quest-giver and the anchor of the office hub. She appears alongside Ducky in optional Act 2 dialogue during the "Love After Life" stage, and she headlines the game's marketing as the company's founder.

## Kit, Zorro, and the Supporting Cast of Plumville

### Kit, the fox shopkeeper

Kit is a fox who helps Mei secure the potion behind her dragon transformation. Kit is also the game's merchant: furniture and decorative items for Ducky's room are purchased from Kit. The in-game storefront is called the Kit Shop, and patch 1.2.1 added new items to it, including Halloween-themed decor.

### Zorro, the masked CEO of OKCO

Zorro is the closest thing the game has to a villain. He leads the corporation OKCO, and his company plans to demolish Mei's shrine for a property development. Mei and Ducky have to stop the demolition while continuing to reunite Plumville residents with their belongings — so the story alternates between cozy fetch-and-find work and a land-development conflict.

### Clients, townspeople, and stage-specific figures

The main story spans eleven chapters, each built around a client seeking help from the company. Finding their requested objects advances that client's story, and characters introduced in earlier chapters can reappear later — a detail reviewers have singled out as one of the game's strengths.

Individual stages also bring their own personalities. The "Fangtastic Feast" stage, added in the October 2026 update, is built around "the famous Count's feast" according to the official update post, complete with a haunted mansion and monsters. Community guides on Steam document optional objectives tied to specific NPCs, but those are player-written and not part of the official character material.

## How Characters Fit Into the Gameplay

Characters in *Lost and Found Co.* are not just talkers — they are targets. Each illustrated isometric stage presents a list of objects **or people** you must locate by examining the scene and clicking on them. Some are hidden behind other objects and only appear after you interact with the environment.

- **Characters as findable targets:** the search list mixes props and people, so you will be clicking on faces as often as on furniture.
- **Characters as quest hooks:** optional objectives are sometimes discovered by interacting with characters or objects mid-stage.
- **Characters as returning faces:** you can revisit the game's fifteen-plus stages to finish optional goals, and earlier clients reappear later in the narrative.
- **Characters in co-op:** the announced Nintendo Switch 2 and Switch versions add co-op for up to 4 players, so multiple people can explore Plumville together when those versions arrive in early 2027.

## FAQ

**Who is the main character in Lost and Found Co.?**

Ducky, a duck transformed into a human boy by the goddess Mei. Ducky works as an intern at Mei's lost-property business and is the character the player controls throughout the story.

**Is Mei a goddess or a dragon?**

Both, at different points. She begins as an ancient war goddess whose power is fading, and the plot summary states she later drinks a potion — obtained with Kit's help — and becomes a small dragon, which is why the store description talks about helping a tiny dragon regain her power.

**Who is the villain in Lost and Found Co.?**

Zorro, the masked chief executive of the OKCO corporation. OKCO wants to demolish Mei's shrine for a property development, and stopping that demolition is the central conflict running alongside the lost-item cases.

**Are there more Lost and Found Co characters than the four main ones?**

Yes. The main story has eleven chapters, and each one centers on a Plumville client who needs help finding lost belongings. Official material does not publish a full named roster of every resident, so most of these townspeople are best discovered in-game rather than from a list.
