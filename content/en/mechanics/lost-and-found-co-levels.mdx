## What Counts as a "Level" in Lost and Found Co

Lost and Found Co is a hidden-object adventure built around cozy, densely decorated locations where you track down lost items, solve small puzzles, and help a tiny dragon regain her power, as described on the [official Lost and Found Co Steam hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390). Because the game never hands you a traditional level-select screen with numbered stages, players often ask what a "level" actually is here.

The answer, based on the [Casual Game Guides walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) published April 19, 2026, is that a level is any self-contained searchable scene you can enter, clear, and later revisit. Those scenes fall into a few recognizable buckets, and knowing which bucket you are in changes how you should search.

- **Story Cases** — the main progression. These move the adventure forward and gate later content.
- **Requests** — side jobs that behave like optional stages with their own item lists.
- **Optional objectives** — extra goals tucked inside a level you are already searching.
- **Juju** — hidden helpers disguised as ordinary objects, caught as an optional objective.

If you only care about finishing the story, you can focus on the main objective and move on. If you want everything, the same scene becomes a cleanup project later, which is why so many Lost and Found Co levels get revisited rather than cleared in one pass.

## The Three Objective Types on Every Stage

Once you are inside a scene, the game tags what it wants from you. Casual Game Guides breaks these into three categories, and each one demands a different searching habit.

| Objective type | What it means | How to approach it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Obscured** | The target is hidden inside or behind something clickable | Open the prop first — drawers, boxes, trays, signs, window frames, shelf lips |
| **Quest** | Finding one item unlocks more targets in a chain | Do not assume the room is finished when the first item disappears |
| **Juju** | A helper disguised as a normal object, counted as an optional objective | Catch them for extra hints on later searches |

### Obscured objectives

When a clue feels strangely vague, the usual cause is that the answer is not visible yet. Many misses in this game come from awkward hitboxes or props that only partially show the item, so clicking the center of small objects and interacting with containers matters more than clicking fast.

### Quest objectives

Quest chains are the reason a room can look "done" while the to-do list still has entries. If a target vanished and nothing new appeared, re-scan the same zone before moving on.

### Juju objectives

Juju are optional, and the walkthrough's advice is to save them. Spending a hint the moment a room frustrates you wastes the resource; use it once you have narrowed the problem to one stubborn object or one stubborn corner.

## How to Clear a Level to 100%

Full completion means clearing the main objective, finishing optional tasks, catching the Juju, and leaving nothing greyed out. According to the [100% completion and achievement list](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/completion-achievements) from April 19, 2026, the then-current Steam build had 27 achievements, each tied to 100% completion of a specific stage — no speedrun tricks and no missable route splits. Because later updates added stages, treat that number as dated rather than final.

The same guide flags the heaviest cleanup runs: Hop Street, Jolly Jug Isle, Nekoten Cafe, Breezy Hill Heights, Leo Family's Home, and Kenta's House. Its recommended route is undramatic but effective:

1. Finish the story once so you learn how each room likes to hide things.
2. Replay stages with unfinished side tasks while the layout is still fresh.
3. Save the clutter-heavy maps for calm sessions instead of forcing perfection on a first visit.
4. Spend Juju only when you are down to the last object or zone.

Search technique carries most of the weight. Break each scene into layers — skyline and hanging props first, then shelves and counters, then floor clutter — and clear one zone or floor before jumping to the next. Zoom in on tiny items, pan across packed areas instead of clicking blind, and remember that the game likes to tuck things into the edges of signs, shelf lips, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes.

## Stage-Specific Quirks and Known Issues

Patch notes give a useful picture of which Lost and Found Co levels have needed fixes. The 1.2.1 patch posted October 14 on the Steam hub lists stage fixes for the Lobby (updated objects around the office, duplicate visitor characters resolved), Love After Life (optional dialogue added to the Chest and for Ducky and Mei during Act 2, plus a stage soft lock fixed), and Fangtastic Feast (a slime objective not displaying correctly when re-entering the stage).

There is also a community-reported bug worth knowing before you start a ninja-themed stage. A Steam guide by 小蓝洗衣机 covering the June 24, 2026 update warns that triggering a shuriken on the tower before accepting the related mission can stop it from counting, potentially blocking level completion. The guide advises talking to the NPC first thing on entering the map. That report was described as submitted and pending a fix at the time of writing, so confirm current behavior in-game.

## FAQ

**How many levels does Lost and Found Co have?**
There is no fixed public number, because the game counts story cases, requests, and side stages separately and has received multiple content updates. Guides from early 2026 list chapters 1–9 plus numerous side stages, and later updates added more maps.

**Do I need 100% completion on every Lost and Found Co level for achievements?**
Not on every stage. The April 2026 achievement breakdown notes that some request-style stages, including Clubhouse of the Dead, The Stumbling Steps, and Duckinator 3029, did not have separate Steam achievements at that time.

**What is the fastest way to clear a difficult stage?**
Search in layers, clear one floor or zone at a time, open clickable props before assuming an item is missing, and hold your Juju hints until only one or two objects remain.

**Can I replay Lost and Found Co levels after finishing the story?**
Yes. Walkthroughs consistently recommend finishing the story first, then returning to stages with unfinished optional objectives and Juju while the room layout is still familiar.
