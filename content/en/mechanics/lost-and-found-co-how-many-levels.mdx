If you’re trying to figure out how many levels are in Lost and Found Co., the clean answer is: the game has 11 main-story chapters and more than 15 hidden-object stages, not one permanent level total. The game separates story chapters from playable stages, and free updates have added new stages since launch. As of November 2, 2026, the safest verified count is “11 chapters, 15+ stages”—with the exact stage number depending on your patch and what you count as a level.

## How Many Levels Are in Lost and Found Co.?

The most reliable way to answer is to separate the game’s structure into categories:

- **Main story chapters:** 11
- **Hidden-object stages:** more than 15
- **Bonus challenge levels:** included, but no official count is listed
- **Side quests and optional objectives:** separate from the main level count

This structure comes from [Wikipedia’s overview of Lost and Found Co.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_and_Found_Co.), which describes eleven chapters and more than fifteen stages that players can revisit. The [official Steam page for Lost and Found Co.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2101390/Lost_and_Found_Co/) also advertises bonus challenge levels without giving a specific number.

That last point matters: “level” is not a formal term the game uses with one fixed definition. If you count every stage, challenge, and side quest, your number will be higher than the story chapter count.

## Chapters vs. Stages: What Counts as a Level?

A chapter is a story segment, usually built around a client who needs help from the Lost and Found Co. A stage is the illustrated isometric scene where you actually search for people and objects.

Because the game has 11 chapters but more than 15 stages, those two numbers do not map one-to-one. The game can also be revisited: players can return to stages to finish optional objectives or restart them.

If you are comparing counts online, check whether the writer means:

- story chapters
- hidden-object stages
- bonus challenge levels
- side quests
- achievements

Those are different lists. Mixing them is the main reason you will see conflicting totals.

## Why You May See a Different Level Count

Steam currently lists 31 achievements for Lost and Found Co., and [BoilingSteam’s March 2026 review](https://boilingsteam.com/lost-and-found-co/) guessed at 27 scenarios based on the achievement list. That guess was speculative, not an official stage count, and it predates later content updates.

Achievements are not levels. They can be tied to story milestones, optional objectives, decorations, or side activities. A 31-achievement list does not mean 31 stages.

The game has also changed since launch. The June 2026 summer update added two stages, “Master of Findjutsu” and “My Summer Memories 2.” The October 1, 2026 update added “Love After Life” and “Fangtastic Feast.” Any fixed total from March 2026 is already out of date.

## Which Stages Are Part of the Main Story?

The main story is made up of eleven chapters. Named scenarios in walkthroughs include “Toy Time Panic,” “What an Unlucky Day?”, “Muddy Situation,” “Smoothie Showdown,” and “The Accursed Antique Shop.” These are story scenarios, not a complete list of every stage.

Side content sits outside that main count. Examples include the Runaway Foxes side quest and the KOZ side quests “Apartment Apocalypse” and “Waiting for a Bite.” Optional objectives can be tracked separately from the main story.

For a clean count, focus on two numbers: 11 chapters for the campaign and more than 15 stages for the playable hidden-object scenes. Treat side quests and bonus challenges as extra content, not as chapters.

## How Long Does It Take to Finish the Levels?

[HowLongToBeat’s user-submitted times](https://howlongtobeat.com/game/152144) give a rough planning range:

- **Main story:** about 8½ hours
- **Main + sides:** about 13 hours
- **Completionist:** about 18 hours

These are player-reported averages, not a guarantee. Your time will depend on how thoroughly you search each stage, how many optional objectives you chase, and whether you use hints.

The level count and playtime are related but not identical. A completionist run takes longer because optional objectives and hidden interactions add work beyond finishing the main story stages.
