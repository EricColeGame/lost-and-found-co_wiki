## What the Balloon Objective Requires

The balloon cleanup in **What an Unlucky Day** is an optional objective, not part of the main story route. According to [Casual Game Guides' Chapter 7 walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/chapter-7-what-an-unlucky-day-walkthrough), you need to find **8 balloons** around the stage.

A few details matter for completion:

- The balloons are separate from the main story items.
- A missed balloon can keep you short of 100% even after you finish the Golden Ticket.
- The stage is one large floor, so the challenge is keeping your search organized rather than moving between floors.
- The walkthrough recommends saving the balloon sweep for last if you want to keep the main item route clean.

*Lost and Found Co.* is a hidden-object adventure, so small props and crowded signboards are part of the design. The [official Steam store page for Lost and Found Co.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2101390/) describes the game as a cozy hidden-object adventure built around finding lost items and solving puzzles.

## A Clean Sweep Route for the Other Six

The available references do not list all eight balloon coordinates. What they do provide is a sensible search order: start on the left side around the water and alley, move through the central shop signs, then finish at the right-side storefronts. That route comes from the Chapter 7 walkthrough, which recommends panning across the stage in that sequence for the balloon cleanup.

Use that route like a checklist:

1. **Start at the far-left water and alley area.** Sweep the waterline and the alley path before moving inward.
2. **Move through the central shop signs.** This is where sign-heavy clutter can hide a balloon, so check sign faces, centers, and frames.
3. **Finish at the right-side storefronts.** Work through the remaining signs and shop fronts before leaving the stage.
4. **Rescan every sign or billboard.** The Steam thread notes that a tricky balloon can blend into a sign rather than sit behind a prop. The donut sign is the clearest example.

This route will not magically reveal the other six balloons, but it prevents the common mistake of half-searching several zones and forgetting which signs you already checked.

## How to Find the Last Balloon

When you are down to one or two balloons, slow down instead of clicking faster. The Chapter 7 guide’s general advice is to scan in sections, zoom in on small objects, and pan across crowded areas rather than clicking blindly. That approach fits the balloon hunt well because the remaining balloon is often tucked into a sign graphic rather than placed in open space.

Try these checks before you move on:

- Recheck the **donut sign**, especially the center of the round donut.
- Recheck the **chicken sign**, since one player found a balloon there.
- Scan sign borders, center graphics, and mascot artwork instead of only the open street.
- Work clockwise or in a fixed route so you do not revisit the same storefront twice.
- Save any hint system for after a full sweep, when you have narrowed the problem to one zone.

The thread’s key takeaway is simple: the last balloon may not be obscured by an object at all. It can blend into a sign or billboard, which makes it easy to look past even when it is technically visible.
