## What a Request Actually Is in Lost and Found Co.

Lost and Found Co. is a hidden object adventure set across magical locations in and around Plumville, and it separates its content into four moving parts. **Story Cases** push the main adventure forward. **Requests** are optional side jobs you pick up outside that main line. **Optional objectives** are extra goals tucked inside a level. **Juju** are hidden little helpers that make future searches easier.

That structure matters because it tells you what a requests walkthrough can and cannot do. No request blocks the story, so you are never forced to grind one out before moving on. But requests are also where the game hides its fussiest objects, and several of them carry their own Steam achievement.

A community achievement breakdown published by [Casual Game Guides' Lost and Found Co. walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/completion-achievements) in April 2026 counted 27 achievements in the then-current Steam build, each tied to 100% completion on a specific stage. Only two request stages had a named achievement in that list: **Waiting for a Bite** and **Tea Time Troubles**.

## How Request Objectives Behave

Most of the frustration in a request stage comes from misreading the objective type rather than from genuinely missing the object. Once you know which of the three you are dealing with, the room gets smaller.

- **Obscured objectives** are hidden inside or behind something clickable. If the clue feels vague, the answer is usually to open the drawer, tray, box, or sign first.
- **Quest objectives** are chained. Finding one item unlocks the next, so a room is not finished just because the first target vanished.
- **Juju** look like ordinary props and function as optional objectives. Catching them grants extra hints for later hunts.

There is also a hitbox factor that casual hidden object players know well. Small items often register best when you click near their center, and partially visible objects still count. A folded bill can pass as a wallet, and a prop wedged into a shelf seam may be the thing the clue was describing all along.

## A Repeatable Search Route for Any Request Stage

The most reliable method is not clicking faster. It is scanning the same room the same way every time, so your eyes stop re-checking places you already cleared.

1. **Work in layers.** Sweep the skyline and hanging props first, then shelves and counters, then floor clutter.
2. **Clear one zone before moving on.** On multi-floor or multi-room maps, finish a zone completely rather than half-searching five areas.
3. **Use the movement tools.** Zoom on anything tiny, pan across packed shelves instead of clicking blind, and switch floors as soon as furniture starts stacking.
4. **Click the props, not just the background.** Signs, shelf lips, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes frequently need one extra click before the real target appears.
5. **Trust shape over wording.** Localization can make an object name sound stranger than the object looks. The art still plays fair.

Hold your Juju until the end. They are most valuable when you have narrowed the problem to one stubborn item in one stubborn corner, not when a whole room still looks untouched.

## Known Bugs, Patches, and Community Guides

Before you assume you are the problem, check your build. The developers posted patch notes for version 1.2.1 on October 14 on the [official Lost and Found Co. Steam community page](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390). That patch fixed a stage soft lock in Love After Life, corrected a slime objective in Fangtastic Feast that failed to display when re-entering the stage, and repaired a To-do UI that would not open after a quest unlocked.

There is also a live mission-counting issue worth knowing about. A Steam guide author credited as 小蓝洗衣机, writing about the June 24, 2026 update, warned that triggering a shuriken on the tower before accepting the related mission can stop it from counting, potentially blocking 100% on that level. The guide's advice is to talk to the NPC as soon as you enter the map. That is a community report, not an official statement, and it was flagged as pending a fix at the time of writing.

All of these are indexed on the [Steam Community guides page for Lost and Found Co.](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390/guides/), which is also where new post-update guides tend to appear first.

## FAQ

**Do I have to complete requests to finish Lost and Found Co.?**
No. Story Cases carry the main adventure. Requests are side jobs, so you can finish the campaign and come back for cleanup whenever you like. The only reason to prioritize them is achievement completion.

**How many achievements are tied to request stages?**
In the April 2026 Steam build, 27 achievements existed and all were tied to 100% completion of a stage. Only Waiting for a Bite and Tea Time Troubles were named request achievements. Achievement lists change with updates, so verify against your current build.

**What is the fastest approach to a Lost and Found Co requests walkthrough?**
Play the story first so you learn how each room hides objects, then replay request stages while the layout is still fresh. Save Juju for the final stubborn object, and use video or screenshot guides only after your own sweep fails.

**Why is an objective not registering even though I found the item?**
Check whether the objective is chained, since some targets only appear after an earlier one is found. If the count still refuses to move, update to the latest patch and check the Steam forums for stage-specific bug reports.
