## Sheriff Beef at a Glance

The Goddess is a Part-Timer! is the Sheriff Beef stage in *Lost and Found Co.*, and it is where a lot of 100% runs stall on a single plate or one very small ninja. The stage carries 13 named objectives, and the achievement of the same name only unlocks once the whole case reads 100%. The work splits into burger orders you assemble at the counter and search tasks scattered across the dining room, the second floor, and the signage outside.

One structural detail worth knowing before you start: the community [main and optional objectives checklist for Lost and Found Co.](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co/main-optional-objectives) files this stage under Request stages rather than the story cases. That matters because Request stages lean harder on optional side tasks, and optional tasks are exactly what keeps the completion bar from filling.

## Working Through the Burger Orders

The burger tasks are the spine of the stage. You are building at the counter where Ducky serves, and each named order (cutest, coolest, polar opposites, undead) is tracked separately. If a burger objective refuses to complete, the usual culprits are the same ones that break hidden-object stages everywhere: a partly obscured prop you have not clicked, or a chain item that spawns new targets after the first one is found.

Two practical habits help here:

- **Finish the burger you started before wandering.** The counter area is dense with props, and half-finished orders blur together fast.
- **Click the container, not just the food.** Shelves, trays, drawers, and boxes often need one extra click before the real target is exposed — the same obscured-object rule that governs the rest of the game, as explained in this [Lost and Found Co. walkthrough primer](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co).

For the delivery objectives (big egg, pink magical girl, octopus friend), the clue text is doing the heavy lifting. Follow the wording literally and sweep the room the boss is standing in before you branch out.

## The Objectives Players Miss Most

### Put Away the Dirty Dishes

This is the most common last-item wall in the stage. Players in a [Steam Community thread on the dirty dishes objective](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390/discussions/0/806844795448382538/) confirmed the set is findable with no bug, but several got stuck at four of five plates. The reported holdout sits on a cart or trolley behind the serving station where Ducky makes the burgers, tucked near the ceiling signs above the barrel juice dispenser.

Reports describe the cart slightly differently — one player puts it at the right end of the serving station, another says to look just left of the dispenser — so sweep both sides. The cart reads as scenery, and at least one player had to click between the cracks to register the plate.

### Find All the Hidden Ninja Boys

The ninja boys are the other classic blocker. In the same Steam thread, players trade these spots:

- Beside the white bat.
- On the second floor, in the corner.
- Where you can only see a leg twitching near Speed grandma.
- Far left, behind the end of the hedge and behind the large burger sign.

If you have three and cannot find the fourth, the outdoor sign at the far left is the one most people overlook.

### The Smaller Sweeps

For the remaining optional tasks, the tips from TPose Gaming's guide are a reasonable starting map: check play areas and seating for the cow plushies, inspect kitchen surfaces and floor tiles for bugs, and keep interacting with the ordering kiosk until it gives way. The frozen guy and the octopus both sit behind interaction puzzles rather than pure spotting, so click anything the cursor highlights even when it looks like decoration.

## Search Strategy That Saves Time

*Lost and Found Co.* rewards layered scanning far more than fast clicking. Clear the scene in bands — hanging props and signage first, then counters and shelves, then floor clutter — and finish one zone before jumping to another. If the restaurant stacks furniture or props over itself, change floors instead of squinting.

Keep the objective labels in mind while you sweep. An **obscured** objective is hidden inside or behind something clickable, so open the prop before deciding the item is missing. A **quest** objective unlocks further targets after the first find, which means the room is not done the moment one chain item disappears.

Hints are best saved for the endgame. TPose Gaming describes a hint button, Dilly's "Divine Sight," that its guide says recharges roughly every minute, and it also notes the game's spatial audio gives a faint chime near quest items. Juju, the hidden helpers covered in general walkthroughs, are optional collectibles that feed you extra hints later — spend them when you are down to one stubborn item, not when a room is merely annoying.

## FAQ

**Does a Lost and Found Co The Goddess Is a Part Timer walkthrough need every optional objective?**
Yes. The achievement of the same name unlocks at 100% completion of the stage, and the optional tasks — dishes, ninja boys, plushies, bugs, the kiosk, the frozen guy — are part of that percentage.

**Where is the last dirty dish at Sheriff Beef?**
Community reports point to a cart behind the serving station where Ducky works, near the ceiling signs above the barrel juice dispenser. Sweep both ends of the counter and click between the cracks; the cart blends into the background.

**Are the dishes or ninja boys bugged?**
Players in the March 6 Steam thread reported no bug for the dishes, with everyone who kept searching eventually finding the full set. The ninja boys were also confirmed findable, including the easily missed one behind the hedge and burger sign on the far left.

**What should I do first in this stage?**
Clear the quick optional sweeps — plushies, bugs, kiosk — then run the burger orders and deliveries. That order matches what the achievement guides recommend and keeps the completion bar moving without long searches interrupting your burger builds.
