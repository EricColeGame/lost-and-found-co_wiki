## What a My Summer Memories 2 Walkthrough Can (and Can't) Tell You

If you are hunting for a Lost and Found Co My Summer Memories 2 walkthrough, the honest answer is that the stage exists, but detailed item-by-item documentation for it is thin. Steam Community guide listings reference **My Summer Memories 2** as a stage with main and side quest objectives, and one guide covering the June 24, 2026 update advertises two new summer maps. What those listings do not give you is a full, verified list of where every object sits.

That matters because the summer farm stage is one of the most crowded in the game. This guide does two things instead of guessing: it explains how to reach the summer content, and it lays out the fully documented item locations for the original **My Summer Memories** stage, which uses the same farm map, the same prop clusters, and the same search logic you will need for its sequel.

## How to Reach the Summer Stages

The summer content is not part of the critical path. According to [Into Indie Games' Chapters 8–9 walkthrough](https://intoindiegames.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co-walkthrough-part-2/), you need to clear side quests to earn enough popularity before stage 8 unlocks, and My Summer Memories is covered alongside Missing Meow and The Accursed Antique Shop in that stretch.

- **Treat it as a side job.** Story Cases push the plot; Requests and side quests build the popularity you need to open later stages.
- **Finish the main objective first.** Clear the story target, then sweep for optional goals once you know the room.
- **Expect a multi-act structure.** My Summer Memories runs through several acts, so a single pass will not clear it.
- **Return with better map knowledge.** Optional goals are far easier on a second visit.

For the broader stage list and update history, the [Lost and Found Co. Steam Community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2101390) is the first-party reference point.

## Search Smarter: Objective Types, Zooming, and Hint Timing

[ Casual Game Guides' Lost and Found Co. walkthrough](https://casualgameguides.com/walkthroughs/lost-and-found-co) makes a point that applies directly to a farm map this dense: clicking faster is not the fix. Layered scanning is.

- **Work in layers.** Skyline and hanging props first, then shelves and counters, then floor clutter. Clear one zone before moving on.
- **Use the movement tools.** Zoom on tiny items and pan across packed areas instead of clicking blind.
- **Open props before giving up.** Many targets are hidden inside signs, shelf lips, window frames, trays, drawers, and boxes, and need one extra click to appear.
- **Understand the labels.** Obscured objectives sit inside or behind something clickable, Quest objectives unlock further targets after the first find, and Juju are optional helpers disguised as ordinary objects.
- **Save hints for last.** Spend a Juju hint when you are down to one stubborn item, not when a room first frustrates you.

That last point matters on multi-act stages. Burning hints early means you have nothing left when the final handful of objects are the only thing between you and a cleared level.

## FAQ

**Do I need to finish My Summer Memories before playing My Summer Memories 2?**
The stages are separate objectives, but both sit in the same summer content block that opens after you build enough popularity through side quests. Clearing the original stage first makes the sequel far easier because you already know the farm layout.

**Why can't I find the bag of blue seeds?**
It moves. It starts on the blue water tank near the crow, the crow takes it, and it reappears between the trees on the right side of the stage before turning up on the pole near the rice paddies.

**How many acts does the summer farm stage have?**
My Summer Memories is described as a long quest with several acts. The walkthrough sources do not state an exact act count, so plan on multiple passes rather than a single sweep.
